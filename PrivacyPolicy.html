Privacy Policy

Match the Shape — Android game ( com.shariqmalik.matchtheshape )

Effective date: 29 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 October 2026

Short version. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information about you. Your high score, sound and vibration settings are saved only on your own device and are never sent to the developer. The only outside service the game talks to is Google AdMob, which shows ads and, in doing so, processes device and ad-related information under Google's own privacy policy.

This policy applies to the Android application Match the Shape (package name com.shariqmalik.matchtheshape), published on Google Play by Shariq Malik (the "developer", "I", "my"). It covers only this app; advertisements shown inside the app lead to third-party products and websites that have their own policies.

2. What the game does not collect

  • No sign-in and no account. The game can be played immediately, without registering anything.
  • No name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address or payment details.
  • No contacts, call log, messages, photos, files, microphone or camera data — the app does not request permission to use any of them.
  • No precise (GPS) location. The app declares no location permission.
  • The developer runs no server and no analytics service of their own, so no usage report, session log or behavioural statistics are sent to the developer.

3. Information stored locally on your device

The game saves a small amount of data on your phone so that it can continue where you left off. This data stays on the device; it is not uploaded, not synced and not visible to the developer.

What is storedWhyWhere
Your best scoreTo show "Best" on the game-over screen and to celebrate new recordsOn this device only
Music, sound-effects and vibration on/off choicesTo remember the settings you pickedOn this device only
A difficulty adjustment value and a count of recent runsTo keep the game fair and gradually ramping for the person actually playingOn this device only
Counters of games played and losses since the last adTo limit how often an interstitial ad is shownOn this device only

Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the game, permanently deletes all of the above.

5. Advertising — Google AdMob

This app displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK, play-services-ads 25.5.0). Ads are the app's only source of revenue.

Two ad formats appear:

  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads shown occasionally after you lose, deliberately spaced out by the in-app counters described above.
  • Rewarded ads — shown only when you tap the "REVIVE" button to continue a run; you choose whether to watch them.

When an ad is requested, shown or clicked, Google's advertising SDK processes information on its own, and under its own policy, including:

  • the Android advertising ID and other resettable identifiers, and ad-attribution signals;
  • device model, hardware characteristics, screen size, language and OS version;
  • network and connection information, and location approximated from your IP address (country / city level — not GPS);
  • read/write of app storage or cookies on your device for ad measurement;
  • interactions with ads (requested, impressions, clicks, video completion, and whether a rewarded ad was earned);
  • crash and performance diagnostics from the app.

Google uses this data for ad selection, delivery, measurement, reporting and fraud prevention, in accordance with its own policies. Because the developer receives no such reports, the developer cannot see or export them.

This app does not contain its own consent screen; consent choices for ad personalisation, where they apply to you, are handled through Google's ad settings rather than inside the game.

7. How data is protected

All network traffic in the app is carried over HTTPS by Google's SDK; the developer's own transfers do not exist because there is no server to transfer to. Locally stored game data is kept in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps; it is not encrypted at rest, which is why only non-sensitive game values are stored there.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Developer-held data: none, so there is nothing for the developer to retain or delete.
  • Local data on your device: delete it at any time by uninstalling the app, or by using Android › Settings › Apps › Match the Shape › Storage › Clear storage.
  • Data processed by Google for advertising: managed through the Google account and ad-settings links in section 5, including Google's Data & privacy controls.
